# Simmer-Logo

Erstellt mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Tool.

Original: `app/static/simmer-logo.png`. Für den Browser-Tab: `app/static/favicon.ico` (16, 32 und 48 Pixel) und `app/static/favicon-32.png`. Weitere Größen: `apple-touch-icon.png` (180 Pixel) und `icon-192.png` (192 Pixel). Die PNGs behalten den transparenten Hintergrund.

## Generierungs-Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: square browser-tab favicon for Simmer, a warm, minimal German recipe archive. Create one bold, beautifully balanced, flat graphic brand mark: a simple cream-colored cooking pot with two short rising steam curls subtly suggesting an S, centered on a solid terracotta #a75739 rounded-square tile. Background outside the rounded square is transparent. Palette taken from the app: terracotta #a75739 and warm cream #f7f3ec. Thick simple shapes with strong negative space, recognizable at 16 by 16 pixels. The tile fills nearly the whole square with just 5 percent transparent margin. No words, letters, wordmark, tiny details, gradients, shadows, photography, mockup, watermark or multiple variants. Output a single centered square icon.
